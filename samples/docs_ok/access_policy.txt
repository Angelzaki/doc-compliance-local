ACCESS CONTROL POLICY
Version 3.2 - Owner: Head of Security

1. Access control principles
Access control at Northwind follows the principle of least privilege, so every employee receives only the permissions needed for their role.
Permissions are granted through role-based access (RBAC) groups defined in the identity provider.
Privileged accounts are limited to the infrastructure team and are reviewed monthly.

2. Access reviews
Managers perform a quarterly access review of all application entitlements.
Reviewers must certify or revoke each entitlement within ten business days.
Deprovisioning of leavers is completed within 24 hours of the offboarding ticket.

3. Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is enforced for all remote access, administrative access and cloud consoles.
Staff use an authenticator app or a hardware security key; SMS codes are accepted only as a fallback.
Single sign-on (SSO) is the default entry point for internal applications.

4. Passwords
Passwords require a minimum length of 14 characters and are checked against known breached lists.
Password reuse across the last 12 passwords is blocked, and accounts enter lockout after 5 failed attempts.
A company password manager is provided to every employee.
