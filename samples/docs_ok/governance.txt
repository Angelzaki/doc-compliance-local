GOVERNANCE AND RISK MANAGEMENT

Change management
Every production change requires change approval after a peer review recorded in a change request ticket.
Changes are tested in staging first, and each release includes a rollback plan.

Vendor management
Procurement runs a vendor risk assessment before onboarding any supplier that handles customer data.
Each vendor signs a data processing agreement, and SOC 2 or ISO 27001 reports are collected and reviewed annually.
Subprocessors are listed in the vendor register.

Security awareness
All staff complete security awareness training during onboarding and every year after that.
Quarterly phishing simulation campaigns measure click rates, and the completion rate is reported to management.

Asset management
The security team maintains an asset inventory of hardware and software in the CMDB.
Assets receive a classification label and lifecycle tracking from purchase to disposal.
