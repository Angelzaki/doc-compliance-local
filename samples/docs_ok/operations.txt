SECURITY OPERATIONS HANDBOOK

Logging and monitoring
Centralized logging collects application, system and network events in a SIEM.
Audit logs are protected from tampering, and log retention is set to 13 months.
Alerts for suspicious activity page the on-call engineer through the monitoring platform, and servers use NTP for time synchronization.

Incident response
The Incident Response Plan defines severity levels, escalation paths and the incident commander role.
Customers and regulators are notified within 72 hours of a confirmed personal data breach.
The response team runs a tabletop exercise twice a year and a post-incident review follows every major event.

Backup and recovery
Daily backups are stored offsite in immutable storage.
A restore test is executed every quarter and the disaster recovery plan sets an RTO of 4 hours and an RPO of 1 hour.

Vulnerability management
Weekly vulnerability scans cover all internet-facing systems.
Critical vulnerabilities must be fixed within 7 days, and critical patches follow the patch management procedure.
An external penetration test is performed annually and findings are ranked by CVSS score with tracked remediation.
