DATA PROTECTION STANDARD

Encryption in transit
All customer traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or TLS 1.3, and HTTPS is mandatory on every public endpoint.
Certificates are issued by an internal certificate authority and renewed 30 days before expiry.
Administrators reach internal networks only through a corporate VPN.

Encryption at rest
Databases, object storage and laptops use encryption at rest with AES-256.
Keys are held in a managed KMS, and key rotation happens every 12 months.
Full disk encryption is mandatory on all employee laptops.

Retention and disposal
Each data category has a documented retention schedule approved by Legal.
Obsolete records are removed through secure deletion, and storage media follow secure disposal procedures with a certificate of destruction.
Personal data is deleted when the retention period ends unless a legal hold applies.
